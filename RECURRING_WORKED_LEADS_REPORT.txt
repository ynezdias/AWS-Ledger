WHAT 'WORKED' MEANS HERE
  A lead counts as worked if ANY of these carry a timestamp:
    last_issued_at     issued to a rep
    last_shopped_at    shopped to a funder
    last_funded_at     funded
    ai_last            AI outreach
    tb_last            TextBolt SMS
    email_out_last     outbound email
    email_in_last      inbound email reply
    task_last          rep task logged
  sf_synced_at is EXCLUDED on purpose - 2.9M leads carry it and it maxes
  out at today, so it is a bulk Salesforce sync stamp, not a human touch.
